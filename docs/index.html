Population-Scale Clinical Validation (N = 854)

Multi-Cohort Clinical Survival Validation of Microvascular Decoupling in EGFR-Mutant Lung Adenocarcinoma (N = 854, p < 10^-15)

Luca Celebrano • School of Medicine and Surgery, Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II & TIGEM

Study Overview

Harmonized multi-center survival benchmarking across 854 primary lung adenocarcinoma patients (TCGA-LUAD + GSE31210): proving that microvascular decoupling truncates median progression-free survival from 78.6 down to 24.0 months.

Read Preprint PDF GitHub Code Master Study Portal

Harmonized Cohort N = 854

Integrates multi-center clinical cohorts with verified molecular and histopathologic profiling (N = 218 verified EGFR-mutant target cohort).

Survival Disparity

Demonstrates extreme progression-free survival divergence: median PFS of 78.6 months in coupled tumors vs 24.0 months in decoupled architectures.

Statistical Power > 97%

Achieves statistical power exceeding 97% at alpha = 0.05, validating microvascular decoupling as a potent independent predictor of early relapse.

Rigorous 5-Tier Audit

Applies strict quality-control filtering to eliminate clinical confounders, ensuring reproducible and un-imputed survival analytics.
